Across the ancient world, trade routes connected distant cities, carrying not only goods but also ideas, knowledge and stories.

Caravans travelled through vast deserts beneath an unforgiving sun. Merchants crossed mountain passes and stopped at busy settlements along the way.

In these cities, markets brought together people from different cultures. Languages, craftsmanship and traditions met in the narrow streets.

The legacy of these journeys can still be seen in the architecture, objects and cultural connections that survived across generations.
